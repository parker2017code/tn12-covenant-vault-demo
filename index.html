Local simulation. No funds move.

Design safe money rules before touching a wallet.

A vault is a simple covenant-shaped app: funds can only leave through known paths, such as a delay, a daily limit, or recovery. This page turns that into a policy artifact builders can critique before real TN12 transaction work starts.

Network lane TN12 / testnet

Toccata and covenant work are not treated here as mainnet-live app rails.
