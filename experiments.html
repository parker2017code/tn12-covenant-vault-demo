Purpose

Show the rule at the spend layer.

The page is not a product catalog. It is a reviewer map for small covenant patterns that can be replayed, checked, or challenged from TN12 evidence.

User meaning

Budgets, assets, and group payments are easier to trust when the accepted and refused moves are visible.

Technical meaning

Covenant IDs, continuation outputs, sibling inputs, and mux routing let small UTXO scripts compose without one app database.

Kaspa angle

Fast TN12 ordering makes multi-transaction covenant flows practical to inspect as workflows, not just final settlement records.

How to read it

Status means evidence class.

Accepted

A TN12 transaction or payload event landed and is linked through an artifact.

Local reject

A candidate failed in the local covenant/script engine. Useful, but not a broadcast rejection.

Indexer-derived

Replay explains state from accepted rows. It does not by itself control funds.
