How to read it

Status means evidence class.

Accepted

A TN12 transaction or payload event landed and is linked through an artifact.

Local reject

A candidate failed in the local covenant/script engine. Useful, but not a broadcast rejection.

Indexer-derived

Replay explains state from accepted rows. It does not by itself control funds.
